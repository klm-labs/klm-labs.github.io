Invoice Creator is an offline tool for creating professional invoice and estimate PDFs on a phone. It is made for independent contractors and tradespeople who want a straightforward way to document a job, share a PDF, and track what they are owed.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Invoice Creator works offline and has no account, cloud sync, or login.

What can I put on an invoice or estimate?

Set up your business name, address, contact details, currency, default tax rate, invoice-number prefix, and optional logo. Save clients, then add line items, quantities, unit prices, dates, tax, and notes or terms. You can create both invoices and estimates.

Can I reuse a document?

Yes. Duplicate an invoice or estimate to make a new draft with a fresh document number and today's date. An estimate can also be converted once into a new invoice; the original estimate remains available.

How do I send a PDF?

Open a document preview and choose Share. Invoice Creator generates the PDF on the device and opens the operating system's share sheet. Choose the mail, messaging, storage, or other destination you prefer. The app does not send messages or email itself.

Does sharing mark an invoice as sent?

No. Sharing does not change a document's status. Use Mark sent when you decide it has been sent, and Mark paid when it has been paid. The home screen shows unpaid invoices and overdue badges for sent invoices with a past due date.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Your business details, clients, documents, and a selected logo stay on your device. To remove the app's local data from a device, uninstall Invoice Creator. The app has no account or server copy to delete.

Will I be able to choose a business-logo photo?

Yes. Choose an image from your device's photo library in Business profile or during first-run setup. If photo access is denied or the image is unavailable, Invoice Creator shows a retry message and saves your other business details without a logo.

Report a bug or request help

Email klm.labs.inc@gmail.com with the following:

  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • The app version and your device model and operating-system version.
  • Steps that reproduce the issue, if possible.
  • A screenshot only if it does not reveal information you do not want to share.

Please do not send invoice PDFs or client details unless they are essential to explaining the issue and you are comfortable sharing them.
