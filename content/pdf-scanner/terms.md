## Check the text

Text recognition reads each page on your iPhone or iPad and can misread words, especially on faint, handwritten, skewed or low-quality pages. Search can miss a word the recognition misread. Check the recognized text, and the scan itself, before you rely on them. The app is not a legal, tax or records-keeping service.

Your documents are stored in the app on your device. We do not hold a copy, so we cannot recover them if your device is lost or reset or the app is deleted, except from your own iPhone or iPad backup. Keep your own copies of anything important, for example with Save to Files.
