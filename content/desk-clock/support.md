# Flip Clock: Big Digital Alarm Support

**Contact:** [klm.labs.inc@gmail.com](mailto:klm.labs.inc@gmail.com)

Flip Clock: Big Digital Alarm (Flip Clock on your Home Screen) turns your iPhone or iPad into a big flip, digital or retro clock for your desk or nightstand, in your colors, with alarms that ring on Silent and in Focus, a 12/24-hour switch, night dimming and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Email us any time; we aim to reply within three business days.

## Common questions

### Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional, and the app works the same without an account. The clock and your alarms work offline. Buying Clock Pro and restoring a purchase need an internet connection.

### Why does the app offer sign-in, and how do I delete my account?

Signing in lets us contact you about the app: if something breaks, or to hear what you think. Tap **Skip** at the top right to go on without signing in; nothing in the app needs it. If you signed in, delete your account any time in **Settings → Account → Delete my account**, or email us and we will delete it. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your Apple Account (see below).

### Do I need to pay?

No. The clock, its three faces, alarms and night mode are free. **Clock Pro**, a one-time purchase, unlocks every other digit color, background and font, red digits at night, and the widgets. The price is shown in the app before you confirm. If you bought Yearly or Lifetime in version 1.0, it keeps working and includes everything in Clock Pro. The full terms are in the [terms of use](/desk-clock/terms/).

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Clock Pro is paid once, so there is nothing to cancel. A Yearly subscription from version 1.0 is cancelled in your Apple Account; deleting the app does not cancel it.

- On your iPhone or iPad, open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, choose the app and tap **Cancel Subscription**.
- Or, in the app, open **Settings → Manage Subscription**.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

### I got a new iPhone or iPad, or reinstalled the app. How do I get my plan back?

Sign in with the Apple Account that bought it, open the app and tap **Restore** on the plans screen, or go to **Settings → Restore Purchases**. Restoring brings back Clock Pro or your plan, not your alarms or look: those are kept on the device where you set them, and in its backups if you have them turned on.

### My plan ended. What happens to my alarms?

Nothing: the clock, its three faces and your alarms are free and keep working. Only the Clock Pro extras lock again, and the clock shows the free look; your saved look comes back when you buy Clock Pro or restore an active purchase.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple: visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in and choose the purchase.

### My alarm didn't ring.

- Open **Alarms** in the app. If it says alarms are off for this app, tap **Open Settings** and allow alarms.
- Check that the alarm is switched on and set for the right days.
- Alarms ring with the system alarm sound at your device's alarm volume. Use **Test alarm** in the alarm editor to hear it.
- A device that is switched off or has an empty battery cannot ring.

### The screen turns off while the clock is showing.

By default the clock keeps the screen on only while your device is charging. Plug it in, or choose another option under **Keep screen on** in **Settings**. Keeping the screen on uses more battery.

### How do I switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time?

Open **Look** and tap **12-hour** or **24-hour**. It starts out matching your device's own time format, and the change applies to the clock, your alarms and the widgets.

### How do I delete an alarm?

Open the alarm in **Alarms** and tap **Delete**. Deleting the app deletes your alarms and settings. There is no server copy, so we cannot recover them.

### What does the app collect?

No analytics event contains your alarm times, repeat days or labels. The app sends usage events with a random identifier, and technical details, to PostHog, and your App Store purchase records to RevenueCat so it can unlock your plan. From version 1.0.1 it also records its screens while you use it: the recordings show your alarm times and repeat days, and mask the labels you type. From version 1.1.0 it sends a crash report to PostHog when the app crashes, and if you sign in, your email address and, if you share it, your name are stored with Google Firebase so we can contact you about the app; they never go to PostHog or RevenueCat. See the [privacy policy](/desk-clock/privacy/).

## Report a problem

Email [klm.labs.inc@gmail.com](mailto:klm.labs.inc@gmail.com) with:

- What happened, and what you expected.
- For an alarm problem, roughly when it should have rung and whether your device was locked, on Silent or in a Focus.
- Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version from **Settings**.
- A screenshot, if it helps.
