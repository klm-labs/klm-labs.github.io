## Alarms: what to expect

Alarms are scheduled with Apple's alarm system (AlarmKit) and ring with the system alarm sound, also when your iPhone is on Silent or in a Focus. An alarm does not ring if the app's alarm access is turned off in Settings, or if your iPhone is switched off or its battery is empty. Use **Test alarm** in the alarm editor to hear how it sounds. Check your alarms before you rely on them, and keep a second way to wake up or be reminded when missing an alarm would matter, for example for travel, work or medicine.

Keeping the screen on uses more battery. If you choose to keep it on while your iPhone is not charging, keep an eye on the battery so it does not run out before an alarm.
