Invoice Creator

Invoices and estimates for contractors and freelancers.

Create an invoice on your phone between jobs, hand the client a clean PDF, and see what you're owed at a glance. No account. No subscription. Nothing to sign up for.

How it works

  • Set up your business once — name, logo, currency, tax rate, invoice numbering.
  • Save a client, add your line items, and watch the total update as you type.
  • Preview the PDF and share it however you like — mail, messages, anything on your phone.
  • Mark it sent, then paid. The home screen keeps a running total of what you're owed.
  • Re-billing a repeat client is two taps: duplicate, share.

Your data stays on your device

Everything lives in a local database on your phone. There is no account and no cloud sync — we never see your invoices, your clients or your totals, and the app works offline because it does not need to be online to do its job.

The app does collect anonymous usage analytics, so we can see which features are used and where people get stuck. It never includes your client details, document contents or amounts, and you can turn it off in Settings → Analytics. The privacy policy explains exactly what it does and does not cover.
