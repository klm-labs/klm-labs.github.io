## Subscriptions

After first-run setup, Invoice Maker needs an active **Invoice Maker Pro** subscription or free trial. It gives you the whole app: creating, editing, previewing and sharing invoices and estimates, and keeping your clients.

**Plans and prices.** There is a yearly plan and a monthly plan. The price, billing period and any free trial are shown in the app before you confirm a purchase. In the United States the plans are US$39.99 a year and US$6.99 a month. Prices in other countries and currencies are set through the App Store or Google Play, may include local taxes, and are the ones shown to you at purchase.

**Free trial.** The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Apple or Google decides eligibility; generally, one trial per store account. When the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and you are charged unless you cancel before then. On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The monthly plan has no free trial.

**Payment and automatic renewal.** Payment is charged to your Apple Account (on the App Store) or your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends. Your subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then-current price unless you cancel it before the renewal date. On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. If a price changes, Apple or Google notifies you, and asks for your agreement where their rules require it.

**Cancelling.** Manage or cancel your subscription in your store account, not in the app:

- **iPhone or iPad:** open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Invoice Maker and tap Cancel Subscription.
- **Android:** open the Google Play Store, go to Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose Invoice Maker and tap Cancel subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. What happens when you cancel during a free trial follows the store’s rules.

**Restoring.** Subscriptions belong to the Apple Account or Google Play account that bought them; Invoice Maker has no account of its own. After reinstalling, or on a new device signed in to the same store account, tap **Restore purchases** on the subscribe screen. A subscription bought on the App Store cannot be used on Android, and a Google Play subscription cannot be used on iPhone or iPad.

**Refunds.** Apple and Google process all payments; KLM Labs never sees your card or bank details and cannot charge or refund your store account directly. For App Store purchases, request a refund from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). For Google Play purchases, follow [Google Play’s refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) or email us, and we will review what we can do through Google Play. Refunds follow the store’s policies and applicable law.

**Checking your subscription.** The app checks your subscription each time it starts. It uses the store’s saved answer when you are offline; if there is none yet, it needs an internet connection to check. Subscribing and restoring also need a connection.

**When a subscription ends.** Your invoices, estimates and clients stay on your device. The next time the app starts, it shows the subscribe screen, and your records stay locked until you subscribe again or restore an active subscription. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Changes to plans.** We may change plans, prices or trial offers for new purchases. A change for existing subscribers is made through Apple or Google under their rules and notices.
