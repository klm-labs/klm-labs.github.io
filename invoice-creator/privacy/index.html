Invoice Creator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-22

Contact: klm.labs.inc@gmail.com

Invoice Creator is made by KLM Labs. It creates invoices and estimates on a device and lets the user share a PDF when they choose. This policy describes the current data practices of the shipped v1 binary; it must be updated before a release changes those practices.

What we collect

For the currently shipped binary, we do not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics, payment information, or any other data from the app. Its updates.url configuration value is unconfigured, so the included expo-updates package does not perform an over-the-air update check. In that configuration, the app has no account system, backend, app-initiated network request, analytics, advertising, or third-party service that receives app data.

The information a user enters in the app can include business details, client names and contact details, invoice and estimate content, dates, notes, and totals. A user may also choose a logo image for the business profile. This information is not collected by us.

Where data lives

App data is stored locally on the user's device in the app's local database. The app is designed to work offline. We cannot view, retrieve, or delete this local data because it is not sent to us.

Expo Updates configuration

The expo-updates package is included for possible over-the-air (OTA) fixes. The live branch is determined by one named configuration value: if updates.url remains unconfigured, no Expo update check occurs and the no-collection statements in this policy and the store forms apply. If updates.url is configured before a release, the app will check with Expo, Inc.'s servers at launch for an applicable OTA update. That HTTPS check carries technical metadata only, such as the runtime-version fingerprint, update channel, and platform. It does not carry invoice data, client data, business or document data, logo images, or other personal data. Before shipping that branch, we will revise this policy and add the applicable Diagnostics / Device-ID, not-linked-to-identity, functionality disclosure in each store form.

Photo-library access

The app lets a user optionally choose one image as a business logo, which appears on the business profile and on generated PDFs. The image is read on the device and stored locally with the business profile; it is never uploaded. Choosing a logo is optional — a user can decline photo-library access, or clear a chosen logo, and continue using every feature of the app. If the image cannot be read, the app says so and saves the profile without a logo.

Sharing PDFs

The app can generate a PDF on the device and open the operating system's share sheet. It does not send email, messages, or PDFs itself. A PDF leaves the device only if the user chooses a destination in that share sheet, such as a mail, messaging, or storage app. Once shared, the chosen destination and recipient handle the PDF under their own privacy practices.

Third parties, analytics, and advertising

In the current updates.url-unconfigured configuration, Invoice Creator does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking, cloud sync, payment processing, or another third-party service that receives app data. The possible Expo Updates branch is described above and must be disclosed before it is enabled for a release.

Children's data

Invoice Creator is not directed to children. In the current updates.url-unconfigured release configuration, we do not collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the app, the data remains only on that device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Data deletion

To remove Invoice Creator's local data from a device, uninstall the app. This removes the app and its locally stored data from that device. Device backups, if enabled by the device owner, are controlled by the operating system and its backup settings, not by Invoice Creator. We hold no copy to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released. Questions about this policy can be sent to klm.labs.inc@gmail.com.
